Representation alignment (RA) has recently emerged as an effective strategy for improving the generative capability of Diffusion Transformers. However, existing methods typically rely on external pretrained representation models or require labor-intensive manual selection of layer pairs for alignment. In this work, we introduce LARA, a novel RA framework that enables flexible alignment across arbitrary layer pairs within a DiT. Our approach is motivated by the fact that different layers in the transformer attend to distinct tasks, capturing information hierarchically. Building on this insight, LARA learns a lightweight, layer-aware transition predictor that shares informative representations throughout the network, effectively bridging the statistical mismatch across layers. This design enables scalable and unified representation alignment throughout the network by flexibly aligning layers to exploit richer internal representations, thereby outperforming previous handcrafted methods without requiring external supervision. Extensive experiments and ablation studies demonstrate that LARA consistently improves generative quality and yields stronger representations for downstream tasks.
