LARA learns transitions between randomly sampled internal layers of a diffusion transformer using one depth- and timestep-conditioned predictor. The overview groups prediction, transition consistency, and layer redundancy as the LARA objectives alongside flow matching. Prediction and redundancy each use one random layer pair per training iteration; the redundancy pair is sampled independently of the prediction pair. The embeddings e_t, e_a, and e_b encode the timestep, source layer, and target layer, respectively. The illustrated SiT-B/2 predictor follows a 768-to-384-to-768 channel path with ten ConvNeXt-style spatial blocks and attention after blocks 4 and 8. Prediction aligns the source-derived state with a stop-gradient target activation. For a < b < c, consistency matches a direct online transition a-to-c to a stop-gradient EMA continuation b-to-c of the cached online prediction a-to-b. Layer diversity penalizes squared cosine similarity between independently sampled layer pairs. sg denotes stop-gradient; LN denotes layer normalization; MHSA denotes multi-head self-attention.
